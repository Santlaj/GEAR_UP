# Ruleset Data — Source-derived completion

This package preserves the existing 95 Legal Metrology records and adds the remaining Legal Metrology material (Rules 25–34 and Schedules I–VII) plus the FSSAI Labelling and Display Regulations, 2020 Version VIII dated 09.09.2025.

Sources used ONLY:
1. The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 — uploaded PDF.
2. Food Safety and Standards (Labelling and Display) Regulations, 2020 — Version VIII (09.09.2025) — uploaded PDF.

Missing/unclear source information is represented as NOT_FOUND_IN_SOURCE where applicable.
All records use verification_status = EXTRACTED_UNVERIFIED.
